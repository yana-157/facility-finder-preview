How results are ranked
  • Every sort requires service confidence of at least 25/100 and excludes branches with a documented fit of zero. Recommended also requires a known fit score. It shows scores of 60–100 first, then 55 to under 60, 50 to under 55, and positive scores under 50. The service match is included in fit.
  • Within a fit range, Recommended adds a confidence penalty to the estimated trip and puts lower totals first. TravelTime estimates trips of up to three hours on a typical weekday morning in your chosen mode. If routing is unavailable, the app labels its straight-line comparison.
  • Ranked criteria applies your chosen priorities in order. Ease pressure on busy facilities can promote a nearby routine-care alternative in the same fit range when the workload data are comparable and the confidence-adjusted detour is small. Equity comparisons require paired evidence for the same branch and service.
